🎯 Stage 1/5

SILENT WATCH

5 stages. You are on a skyscraper roof above downtown. Command describes the suspect over the radio. Eliminate the target and do not kill any civilian. Later stages are busier, faster and darker.

Walk around the roof with the stick (or WASD) to the edge, then double-tap the screen (or press Climb / C) to jump onto the barrier and look down at the street. Drag (or arrows) to aim · Zoom for scope · Hold breath to steady · Space fire, Z zoom, V view, Shift breath
